Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about laser hair reduction at Ubercare Bling.

Laser hair reduction is an aesthetic procedure that uses concentrated light pulses to target the pigment (melanin) inside hair follicles. The absorbed thermal energy disables follicle activity without harming surrounding dermal tissue, resulting in progressive, long-term hair reduction over a series of sessions.
Most adults can safely undergo laser hair reduction. With modern multi-wavelength technology, a broad spectrum of skin tones and hair densities can be treated effectively. However, suitability is verified during your initial consultation to account for factors like active skin conditions, pregnancy, or photosensitizing medications.
Treatment plans vary depending on factors such as hair type, skin type, treatment area and individual biological response. Because hair grows in cycles (anagen, catagen, telogen), laser is most effective during the active growth stage. Most clients complete a course of 6 to 8 sessions spaced several weeks apart to achieve optimal reduction.
Yes. Areas such as the upper lip, chin, jawline, sideburns, and cheeks can be treated safely. Facial treatments use specialized smaller applicators and precise energy settings to protect delicate skin and eye zones.
We recommend shaving the targeted area 24 hours prior to your session. Avoid waxing, threading, plucking, or bleaching for 3–4 weeks beforehand, as the root must remain intact in the follicle. Keep the skin moisturized and avoid excessive sun exposure or tanning prior to treatment.
Mild redness or slight warmth similar to a gentle sun sensation is normal and typically subsides within a few hours. You can resume daily activities right away. We advise avoiding hot showers, saunas, and strenuous exercise for 24 hours, and applying broad-spectrum sunscreen on exposed areas.
